# Stacks Collectibles & Goods

Buy, sell, trade items and collectibles, plus more
An independent online marketplace for trading cards, coins, die-cast vehicles, vintage media, and curated secondhand goods—built in Tempe, Arizona.

🌐 **Live Storefront:** [Stacks Collectibles & Goods](https://dustinstack8181-tech.github.io/Stacks-Collectibles-and-Goods/)

---

## What We Offer
* **Trading Cards:** Single cards, vintage sets, and curated lots across Pokémon, Final Fantasy TCG, Cardfight!! Vanguard, Weiß Schwarz, Dragon Ball Z, Magic: The Gathering, Marvel, and vintage sports cards.
* **Coins & Currency:** U.S. silver coinage, wheat cents, proof sets, and vintage paper notes.
* **Die-Cast & Collectibles:** Unopened Hot Wheels, NASCAR die-cast, action figures, Funko Pop! figures, and physical media.
* **Audio & Goods:** Select car/home audio hardware and unique local finds.

## Platform Features
* **Browse & Share Listings:** Clean storefront views with quick sharing and item details.
* **Member Accounts:** User registration, sign-in, and profile management powered by Supabase.
* **Admin Controls:** Live storefront customization, listing management, and member approval workflows.

## Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (hosted on GitHub Pages)
* **Backend & Auth:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Authentication, Row Level Security)
